## IT 630: Data Science and Scalable Data Systems

- Source lineage: `it630-syllabus-brief#sections.course`
- Citations: `syllabus_offering`

### Fall 2026 · 4 credits · Computer Science

Required for Analytics and Data Science majors. Elective for Computer Science and Information Technology majors, and for Data Science and Analytics minors. Not writing intensive.

- **What**: Build the data foundation that dashboards, machine learning, and AI applications actually run on.
- **How**: Acquire, shape, query, validate, and serve data, and be able to defend the number at the end of it.
- **Then**: Diagnose why two defensible analyses disagree: grain, missingness, leakage, denominators, and definition drift.
